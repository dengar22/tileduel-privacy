Privacy Policy

Effective Date: February 24, 2026

TileDuel ("the App") is developed and operated by Joshua Raef ("we," "us," or "our"). This privacy policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, your rights regarding that data, and how to contact us.

1. Data We Collect

Guest users (no account): If you play without creating an account, no personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted. All game data stays entirely on your device.

Registered users (online multiplayer): If you create an account to use online features, we collect and process the following data:

  • Email address — used for account authentication, password recovery, and essential account communications.
  • Display name — the name you choose to show to other players in online games.
  • Game data — board state, scores, moves, and game outcomes for online multiplayer games you participate in.
  • Basic usage statistics — games played, wins, and best word score, stored with your account profile.

We do not collect any data beyond what is listed above. We do not use cookies, analytics SDKs, advertising identifiers, or any form of cross-app tracking.

2. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and United Kingdom, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance — Processing your email, display name, and game data is necessary to provide the online multiplayer service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interest — We retain basic game statistics to provide you with your play history and improve the service.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the online multiplayer game experience:

  • To authenticate your account and let you sign in across sessions.
  • To match you with other players and manage online games.
  • To display your chosen display name and scores to opponents during games.

We do not use your data for marketing, profiling, automated decision-making, or any purpose other than operating the game.

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or any device sensor data.
  • We do not use analytics SDKs, tracking pixels, or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not sell, share, rent, or provide your data to any third parties for advertising, marketing, or any other commercial purpose.
  • The App contains no ads and no ad-related tracking of any kind.
  • We do not engage in cross-app or cross-site tracking.

5. Data Storage and International Transfers

Account and game data is stored using Google Firebase (Realtime Database and Authentication), operated by Google LLC. Firebase servers are located in the United States.

If you are located outside the United States (including in the EEA or UK), your data will be transferred to and processed in the United States. Google maintains appropriate safeguards for international data transfers, including Standard Contractual Clauses. For more information, see Firebase Privacy and Security.

6. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service we use is Google Firebase for authentication and data storage. Firebase processes your data on our behalf and is obligated to protect it in accordance with their data processing terms. We do not share your data with any other third-party services.

7. Data Retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, all associated personal data (email, display name, game history, and statistics) is permanently deleted within 30 days. Anonymized, aggregate game statistics (not linked to any individual) may be retained.

8. Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data directly within the App by going to Settings → Delete Account. Account deletion is permanent and removes your email address, display name, game history, and all associated data. You may also request account deletion by contacting us at the email address below.

9. Your Rights

All users have the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Delete your account and all associated data (via in-app settings or by contacting us).
  • Stop using online features at any time and play entirely offline as a guest with no data collection.

EEA/UK residents (GDPR) additionally have the right to:

  • Request restriction of processing of your personal data.
  • Request portability of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): We do not sell or share your personal information as defined under the California Consumer Privacy Act. You have the right to know what personal data we collect, to request deletion, and to not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights. Because we do not sell personal information, a "Do Not Sell" opt-out is not applicable.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). Account creation requires an email address and is intended for users aged 13 and older. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us immediately and we will delete the account and all associated data promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
